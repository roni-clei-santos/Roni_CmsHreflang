# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Roni Clei Jesus dos Santos LTDA** ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in connection with our website ([ronicleisantos.com.br](https://ronicleisantos.com.br)) and our Adobe Commerce / Magento 2 extensions distributed through the Adobe Commerce Marketplace and GitHub.

> **Summary for Adobe Commerce / Magento 2 Extensions:** Our extensions—including **CMS Hreflang for Multi-Store SEO**—execute entirely within your own Adobe Commerce or Magento Open Source server environment. They do not collect, transmit, track, or store personal data, customer data, or store configuration data on any external servers.

## 1. Data Collection in Adobe Commerce / Magento 2 Extensions

When you install and use our Magento 2 / Adobe Commerce modules:

- **No External Telemetry or Tracking:** The extension does not send usage analytics, store URLs, customer records, or order data to us or to third parties.
- **Local Processing Only:** All configuration values and storefront outputs (such as `hreflang` alternate link tags) are read from and processed locally within your Magento database and store views.
- **Open Source Transparency:** You can inspect the complete source code on [GitHub](https://github.com/roni-clei-santos/Roni_CmsHreflang).

## 4. Data Retention & Your Rights

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Support messages are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry or comply with legal obligations.
